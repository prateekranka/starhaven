TUTORIAL

A quiet mesa. No rival army — only your first Town Center, a patch of lumenfruit, and the Bright Line sliding across the stone.

  1. Drag-select your weavers (villagers).
  2. Tap a glowing fruit cluster to gather food.
  3. Select a villager, choose House, tap the ground to place it.
  4. Select the Town Center and queue more villagers.
  5. Raise a Barracks, train guards, and learn attack-move.
